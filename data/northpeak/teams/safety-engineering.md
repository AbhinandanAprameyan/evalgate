## How to work with us
Open a request in the Safety Engineering channel in company chat, or email safety-engineering@northpeak.example. Urgent requests should mention 'urgent' in the subject line. We try to reply within one business day.

## Team rituals
Weekly planning on Mondays, a demo every second Thursday, and a retrospective at the end of each month.
