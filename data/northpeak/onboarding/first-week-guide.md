# Your First Week at Northpeak

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** People Team  
**Version:** 2026.2  
**Effective date:** July 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Internal


## Day 1
- Collect your laptop and badge (on-site) or receive them by courier (remote).
- Set up your accounts: see the Accounts Setup Checklist.
- Meet your manager and your onboarding buddy.

## Days 2 to 5
- Complete the required security and safety training in the learning portal within your first five days.
- Enroll in health benefits (you have 30 days from your start date).
- Visit the robotics lab or manufacturing floor tour, if you are on-site.

## Remote work
New employees work on-site or follow their team's schedule. Remote and hybrid work become available after 90 days.

## Questions

Contact the People Team at people@northpeak.example. This policy is reviewed once a year.
