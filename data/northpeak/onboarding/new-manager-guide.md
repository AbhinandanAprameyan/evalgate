# Guide for New Managers

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** People Team  
**Version:** 1.3  
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Internal


## Your new approval responsibilities
As a manager you approve time off, expenses up to $2,000, and learning budget requests for your team. Expenses above $2,000 go to a director.

## Reviews and promotions
You will write reviews in April and October and may nominate team members for promotion in the October cycle.

## Hiring
Managers may not receive referral bonuses for hiring into their own team.

## Questions

Contact the People Team at people@northpeak.example. This policy is reviewed once a year.
