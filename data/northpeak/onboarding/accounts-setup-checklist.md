# Accounts Setup Checklist

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** IT  
**Version:** 1.8  
**Effective date:** April 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Internal


1. Sign in to your laptop with the temporary password from IT, then set a new password of at least 14 characters.
2. Install the authenticator app and turn on MFA for your company account.
3. Install VaultKey, the company password manager.
4. Sign in to email, chat and the HR portal.
5. Install the VPN client and test it from home.
6. Request access to team tools (such as the Fleet Console) through an IT ticket.

## Questions

Contact IT at it@northpeak.example. This policy is reviewed once a year.
