# Guide for Remote Employees

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** People Team  
**Version:** 2026  
**Effective date:** July 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Internal


## Equipment
IT ships your laptop and accessories. Return them using the prepaid label if you leave Northpeak.

## Stipends
You receive the home office stipend ($500 one-time) and the internet stipend ($50 per month). If you live more than 50 miles from an office, you may expense a coworking space up to $150 per month.

## Holidays and help desk
Remote employees follow the all-company holiday list plus one floating holiday, and use the help desk hours of the office closest to them.

## Visiting an office
Book a desk in the Facilities portal before visiting Boston or Austin.
