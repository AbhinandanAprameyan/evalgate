# Austin (Manufacturing and Operations) Emergency Procedures

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** Environment, Health and Safety  
**Version:** 2026  
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Internal


- **Fire alarm:** leave by the nearest stairs (never the elevators) and go to the assembly point at the north end of parking lot B.
- **Medical emergency:** call 911 first, then building security at ext. 3300.
- **Robot incident:** press the nearest emergency stop, keep people away, and call security at ext. 3300.
- **Severe weather:** follow announcements from the floor wardens.
