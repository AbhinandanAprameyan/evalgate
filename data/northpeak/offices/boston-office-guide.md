# Boston (Headquarters) Office Guide

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** Facilities  
**Version:** 2026  
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Internal


## Address
225 Harborview Drive, Floors 4 to 7, Boston, Massachusetts (Eastern Time)

## Building access
Badge access: 6:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m., Monday to Friday. After-hours access requires approval from Facilities.

## Parking and commuting
There is no company parking. Northpeak pays 50% of a monthly MBTA pass, up to $100 per month.

## Visitors
Register visitors in the Facilities portal at least 24 hours in advance. Visitors must sign a non-disclosure agreement at reception.

## Local notes
The robotics lab is on Floor 5. Safety glasses and closed-toe shoes are required in the lab.

## Questions

Contact Facilities at facilities-boston@northpeak.example. This policy is reviewed once a year.
