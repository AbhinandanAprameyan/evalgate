# Engineering On-Call Policy

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** Engineering  
**Version:** 2.0  
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Internal


## Rotation
Firmware and cloud engineers join the on-call rotation after six months at Northpeak. Each shift lasts one week, Monday 9:00 a.m. to the following Monday 9:00 a.m.

## Compensation
On-call engineers receive a stipend of **$300 per week** on call, plus one day off after any week with a night-time incident.

## Response times
Acknowledge a SEV1 page within **15 minutes**, a SEV2 within 1 hour, and SEV3 issues by the next business day.

## Revision history
- 2.0 (January 1, 2026): Stipend increased from $200 to $300 per week.

## Questions

Contact Engineering Operations at eng-ops@northpeak.example. This policy is reviewed once a year.
