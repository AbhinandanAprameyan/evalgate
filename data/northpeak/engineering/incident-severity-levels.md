# Incident Severity Levels

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** Engineering  
**Version:** 1.5  
**Effective date:** June 1, 2025  
**Classification:** Internal


| Level | Meaning | Example | Response |
|---|---|---|---|
| SEV1 | Robots unsafe, or many customer fleets down | Robots ignoring emergency stops | Page on-call, acknowledge within 15 minutes |
| SEV2 | One customer fleet down, or a major feature broken | Fleet Console unavailable for a customer | Acknowledge within 1 hour |
| SEV3 | Minor issue with a workaround | Wrong battery icon in the app | Next business day |

Any incident involving possible injury is always SEV1, even if only one robot is affected.

## Questions

Contact Engineering Operations at eng-ops@northpeak.example. This policy is reviewed once a year.
