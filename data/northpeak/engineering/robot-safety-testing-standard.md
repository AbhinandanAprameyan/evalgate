# Robot Safety Testing Standard

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** Safety Engineering  
**Version:** 2.1  
**Effective date:** August 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Internal


## Required tests
Before any robot model or major firmware version ships, Safety Engineering must sign off on emergency stop tests, obstacle detection tests at full speed, and payload limit tests at 110% of rated payload.

## Lift inspection interval (Hauler family)
After the safety committee review in July 2026, lift actuators on NP-300 and NP-350 robots must be inspected every **250 operating hours**. Customer maintenance guides have been updated to match.

## Records
Test results are stored for seven years.
