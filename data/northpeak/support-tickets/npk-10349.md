# Support Ticket NPK-10349

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Date:** 2025-12-28  
**Customer:** Bluefield Grocers  
**Product:** NP-150 Courier Plus  
**Status:** Closed  
**Classification:** Internal

## Customer message
Our NP-150 keeps showing E103 during the morning shift.

## Resolution
E103 on a Courier robot means: Lost localization (cannot find its position on the map). Drive the robot manually to a known map marker and select Relocalize in the Fleet Console.
