**Company:** Northpeak Robotics

**Product:** NP-150 Courier Plus  
**Status:** Closed  
**Classification:** Internal

## Customer message
Our NP-150 keeps showing E104 during the morning shift.

## Resolution
E104 on a Courier robot means: Wheel motor stall. Power the robot off, check the wheels for wrapped plastic or strapping, and restart. Contact support if the code returns.
