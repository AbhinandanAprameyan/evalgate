**Product:** NP-550 Picker Pro  
**Status:** Closed  
**Classification:** Internal

## Customer message
Our NP-550 keeps showing E101 during the morning shift.

## Resolution
E101 on a Picker robot means: Gripper vacuum pressure low. Check the suction cups for tears and the vacuum hose for disconnections; replace worn gripper pads.
