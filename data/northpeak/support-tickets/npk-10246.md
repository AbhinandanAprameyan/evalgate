**Product:** NP-300 Hauler

## Customer message
Our NP-300 keeps showing E103 during the morning shift.

## Resolution
E103 on a Hauler robot means: Lost localization (cannot find its position on the map). Drive the robot manually to a known map marker and select Relocalize in the Fleet Console.
