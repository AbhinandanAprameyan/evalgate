# NP-300 Hauler: Cleaning Guide

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** Customer Support  
**Version:** 2026  
**Effective date:** July 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Public


Clean the NP-300 weekly with a dry or slightly damp microfiber cloth. Never spray water or cleaning liquid directly on the robot; it is rated IP44. Clean the lidar window and camera lenses with a lens cloth. Wipe the charging contacts on the robot and dock monthly with isopropyl alcohol. In food or medical environments, use only disinfectants listed in the approved chemicals table in the Fleet Console help pages.

Applies to: NP-300 Hauler (Hauler family).
