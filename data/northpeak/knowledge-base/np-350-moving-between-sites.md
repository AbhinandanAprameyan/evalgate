# NP-350 Hauler XL: Moving a Robot to Another Site

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** Customer Support  
**Version:** 2026  
**Effective date:** July 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Public


To move a NP-350 to another building, put it in Transport mode from the Fleet Console, power it off, and reinstall the red transport locks. Robots weighing more than 50 kg must be moved with a pallet jack or forklift using the marked lifting points. At the new site, create a new map; maps cannot be copied between sites. Update the robot's site assignment in the Fleet Console so that support cases are routed correctly.

Applies to: NP-350 Hauler XL (Hauler family).
