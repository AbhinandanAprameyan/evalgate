# NP-500 Picker: Mapping Best Practices

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** Customer Support  
**Version:** 2026  
**Effective date:** July 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Public


Create the map of the site when the floor looks the way it does during normal work. Drive the NP-500 slowly (below 0.5 m/s) in Mapping mode, close loops by returning to your starting point, and avoid mapping when large temporary items such as pallets of seasonal stock are present. Add reflective map markers every 30 meters in long, repetitive aisles. After major layout changes, remap only the affected zone using Partial Mapping in the Fleet Console instead of remapping the whole site. Keep a backup of every map version; the Fleet Console stores the last ten versions automatically.

Applies to: NP-500 Picker (Picker family).
