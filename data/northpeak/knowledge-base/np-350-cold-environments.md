# NP-350 Hauler XL: Operating in Cold Environments

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** Customer Support  
**Version:** 2026  
**Effective date:** July 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Public


The NP-350 is designed for indoor temperatures between 5°C and 40°C. In cooler areas runtime decreases: at 5°C, expect about 25% less than the typical 7 hours. Robots should not be used in freezers. Condensation can form when a robot moves from a cold room into a warm, humid area; if this happens, let the robot rest for 30 minutes before charging.

Applies to: NP-350 Hauler XL (Hauler family).
