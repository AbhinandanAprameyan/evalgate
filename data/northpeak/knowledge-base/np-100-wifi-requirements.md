# NP-100 Courier: Wi-Fi Requirements

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** Customer Support  
**Version:** 2026  
**Effective date:** July 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Public


Courier robots need a 5 GHz Wi-Fi network with at least -67 dBm signal strength across the whole operating area. Roaming between access points must be enabled. Captive portals (login web pages) are not supported. If the site changes its Wi-Fi name or password, update every robot from the Fleet Console before the change, otherwise robots may show E103 and stop. For large sites, ask your IT team to run a site survey; Northpeak can provide a survey checklist on request. Robots use about 2 Mbps of bandwidth each during normal operation and up to 20 Mbps while downloading firmware, so schedule firmware updates outside peak shifts.

Applies to: NP-100 Courier (Courier family).
