# NP-350 Hauler XL: Battery Care and Storage

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** Customer Support  
**Version:** 2026  
**Effective date:** July 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Public


The NP-350 battery lasts longest when it is kept between 20% and 90% charge. Opportunity charging between tasks is safe. For storage longer than two weeks, charge to about 60%, power the robot off, and store it between 10°C and 30°C. Do not store robots in freezers or outdoors. Battery capacity normally decreases over time; replace the battery after 4 years or 2,000 charge cycles, whichever comes first. The battery warranty is 1 year, which is shorter than the robot warranty.

Applies to: NP-350 Hauler XL (Hauler family).
