## Location-specific holidays
- **Boston office:** closed on **Patriots' Day, Monday, April 20, 2026**.
- **Austin office:** open on Patriots' Day. Austin employees receive **one floating holiday** instead, to be used by December 31.
- **Remote employees:** follow the all-company list and receive one floating holiday, like Austin.

## Manufacturing shifts
The Austin manufacturing floor runs a reduced shift on the day after Thanksgiving. Shift workers who work that day receive holiday pay at 1.5 times their normal rate.
