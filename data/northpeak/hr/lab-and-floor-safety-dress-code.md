# Lab and Manufacturing Floor Safety Rules

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** Environment, Health and Safety  
**Version:** 4.2  
**Effective date:** February 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Internal


## Dress code on the floor
On the Austin manufacturing floor and in the Boston robotics lab, everyone must wear closed-toe shoes and safety glasses. Loose clothing, scarves and dangling jewelry are not allowed near moving robots. Long hair must be tied back.

## Working near robots
- Stay outside the yellow safety lines while a robot is running.
- Never disable a light curtain or safety sensor.
- Only certified staff may enter a robot cell during testing.

## Reporting incidents
Report every injury or near miss to the shift supervisor immediately, and file a safety report within 24 hours in the EHS portal.

## Questions

Contact Environment, Health and Safety at safety@northpeak.example. This policy is reviewed once a year.
