# Parental Leave Policy

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** People Team  
**Version:** 3.0  
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Internal


## Who is eligible
All full-time employees who have worked at Northpeak for at least **6 months** are eligible, regardless of gender or how they become a parent (birth, adoption or foster placement).

## How much leave
Eligible employees receive **16 weeks of fully paid parental leave**. Leave may be taken all at once or split into up to three blocks, but must be completed within **12 months** of the birth or placement.

## Returning to work
Employees may return part-time (three days a week) for up to four weeks after their leave at full pay. Your role, or an equivalent role, is guaranteed on your return.

## Revision history
- 3.0 (January 1, 2026): Paid leave increased from 12 weeks to 16 weeks.

## Questions

Contact the People Team at people@northpeak.example. This policy is reviewed once a year.
