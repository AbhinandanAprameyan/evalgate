# Performance Reviews and Promotions

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** People Team  
**Version:** 3.1  
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Internal


## Review cycle
Performance reviews happen twice a year, in **April and October**. Employees complete a self-review, managers collect peer feedback, and each employee receives a rating from 1 (does not meet expectations) to 5 (far exceeds expectations).

## Promotions
Promotions are decided **once a year, in the October cycle only**. Managers nominate employees; a calibration committee in each department reviews nominations. Off-cycle promotions are allowed only for retention cases approved by a vice president.

## Salary changes
Merit increases take effect on January 1 following the October cycle. Salary ranges for each level are Restricted information and are managed by the People Team.

## New employees
Employees who joined less than three months before a review are not rated in that cycle.

## Questions

Contact the People Team at people@northpeak.example. This policy is reviewed once a year.
