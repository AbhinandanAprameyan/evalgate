# Employee Referral Program

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** Talent Acquisition  
**Version:** 3.0  
**Effective date:** January 1, 2025  
**Classification:** Internal


## Bonus amounts
When someone you refer is hired, you receive a referral bonus:
- **$3,000** for engineering roles (software, firmware, hardware, robotics),
- **$1,500** for all other roles.

The bonus is paid after the new hire completes **90 days** of employment and you are still employed at Northpeak.

## Who is not eligible
Managers referring someone into their own team, recruiters, and executives are not eligible. Interns and contractors who are rehired as employees do not count as referrals.

## How to refer
Submit the candidate in the careers portal before they apply. Referrals submitted after the candidate has already applied are not eligible.
