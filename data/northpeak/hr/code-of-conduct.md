# Code of Conduct

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** Legal and People Team  
**Version:** 6.0  
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Internal


## Our standards
Every Northpeak employee is expected to act with honesty, treat colleagues and customers with respect, and follow the law in every country where we operate.

## Conflicts of interest
Disclose any outside job, investment or family relationship that could influence your decisions at work. Gifts from customers or suppliers worth more than $100 must be declined or reported to Legal.

## Speaking up
Report concerns to your manager, the People Team, or the confidential ethics line at **1-800-555-0142**, available 24 hours a day. Reports can be anonymous. Northpeak does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Harassment
Harassment and discrimination of any kind are prohibited. See the Anti-Harassment Policy for how investigations work.

## Questions

Contact Legal at legal@northpeak.example. This policy is reviewed once a year.
