# Working Hours and Overtime

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** People Team  
**Version:** 2.0  
**Effective date:** April 1, 2025  
**Classification:** Internal


## Salaried employees
Salaried employees are not paid overtime. Standard working hours are flexible, but employees should be reachable during core hours.

## Hourly employees
Hourly employees, including the Austin manufacturing and warehouse teams, are paid **1.5 times their regular rate for hours worked over 40 hours in a work week**, in line with federal law. Overtime must be approved in advance by a shift supervisor, except in emergencies.

## Breaks
Hourly employees working a shift of six hours or more receive a paid 15-minute break and an unpaid 30-minute meal break.

## Questions

Contact the People Team at people@northpeak.example. This policy is reviewed once a year.
