# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** People Team  
**Version:** 5.1  
**Effective date:** July 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Internal


## Eligibility
Employees become eligible for remote or hybrid work after completing **90 days** of employment. Manufacturing, lab and facilities roles in Austin and Boston require on-site work and are not eligible.

## Core hours
Remote and hybrid employees must be available during core hours of **10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. in their local time zone**.

## Working from another country
Employees may work from outside their home country for up to **2 weeks per calendar year**, with written approval from a director at least two weeks in advance. Longer stays create tax and legal obligations and are not allowed.

## Home office support
- A one-time home office stipend of **$500** for desk, chair or monitor purchases, claimed within 60 days of becoming remote.
- A monthly internet stipend of **$50**, paid through payroll.
- Remote employees who live more than 50 miles from a Northpeak office may expense a coworking space up to **$150 per month**.

## Equipment
Company laptops and accessories are shipped by IT. Do not buy computers personally and expense them.

## Revision history
- 5.1 (July 1, 2026): Coworking reimbursement added.
- 5.0 (January 1, 2025): Home office stipend increased from $300 to $500; internet stipend added.
