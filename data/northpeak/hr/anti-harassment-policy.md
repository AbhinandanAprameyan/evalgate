# Anti-Harassment and Reporting Policy

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** People Team  
**Version:** 2.4  
**Effective date:** June 1, 2025  
**Classification:** Internal


## What is prohibited
Unwelcome conduct based on a protected characteristic, sexual harassment, bullying, and threats are prohibited at work, at company events, and on company chat tools.

## How to report
1. Tell your manager or any member of the People Team, or
2. Call the ethics line at **1-800-555-0142** (anonymous if you prefer), or
3. Email conduct@northpeak.example.

## Investigations
The People Team acknowledges every report within two business days and aims to complete investigations within 30 days. Both parties are told the outcome, although disciplinary details stay confidential.

## Questions

Contact the People Team at people@northpeak.example. This policy is reviewed once a year.
