# Bereavement and Jury Duty Leave

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** People Team  
**Version:** 1.3  
**Effective date:** March 1, 2025  
**Classification:** Internal


## Bereavement leave
Employees may take paid bereavement leave after the death of a family member:
- **5 days** for an immediate family member (spouse or partner, child, parent, sibling, grandparent or grandchild).
- **2 days** for an extended family member (aunt, uncle, cousin, in-law).

Bereavement days do not need to be taken consecutively, but must be used within three months.

## Jury duty
Northpeak pays your regular salary for up to **10 days** of jury duty per year. Give your manager a copy of the jury summons as soon as you receive it. Beyond 10 days, jury duty is unpaid but your job is protected.
