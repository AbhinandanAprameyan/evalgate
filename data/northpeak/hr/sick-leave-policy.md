# Sick Leave Policy

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** People Team  
**Version:** 2.1  
**Effective date:** January 1, 2025  
**Classification:** Internal


## Allowance
All employees receive **10 days** of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave does not carry over and is not paid out when you leave.

## Using sick leave
Sick leave can be used for your own illness or medical appointments, or to care for an immediate family member. Tell your manager as early as possible on the day you are absent.

## Documentation
A note from a healthcare provider is required only after **3 consecutive days** of absence. You never need to share your diagnosis with your manager.

## Longer absences
For absences longer than two weeks, contact the People Team about short-term disability coverage.

## Questions

Contact the People Team at people@northpeak.example. This policy is reviewed once a year.
