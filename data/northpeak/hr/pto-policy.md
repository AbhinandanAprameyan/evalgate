# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** People Team  
**Version:** 4.0  
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Internal


## Purpose
This policy explains how full-time employees earn and use paid time off for vacation and personal needs. Sick leave is covered separately in the Sick Leave Policy.

## How much PTO you earn
PTO accrues every pay period based on your length of service:

| Years of service | PTO per year |
|---|---|
| 0 to 2 years | 15 days |
| 3 to 5 years | 20 days |
| 6 or more years | 25 days |

Part-time employees accrue PTO in proportion to their scheduled hours. Contractors and interns do not accrue PTO.

## Requesting time off
Submit requests in the HR portal. Requests for 3 or more consecutive working days must be submitted at least two weeks in advance. Your manager approves requests; during the last two weeks of a quarter, customer-facing teams may limit how many people are out at once.

## Carryover
You may carry over up to **5 days** of unused PTO into the next calendar year. Any PTO above 5 days that is unused on December 31 is forfeited. Carried-over days must be used by March 31.

## Leaving the company
Accrued, unused PTO is paid out when employment ends, except where local law requires otherwise.
