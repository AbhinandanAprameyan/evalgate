# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** Policy Archive  
**Version:** 3.2  
**Effective date:** January 1, 2024  
**Classification:** Internal

> **ARCHIVED:** This version has been replaced by a newer policy. Do not use it for current decisions.


You may carry over up to **10 days** of unused PTO into the next calendar year. PTO accrues at 15 days per year for 0 to 2 years of service, 20 days for 3 to 5 years, and 25 days for 6 or more years.
