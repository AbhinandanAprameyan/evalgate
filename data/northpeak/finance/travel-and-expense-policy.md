# Travel and Expense Policy

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** Finance  
**Version:** 7.0  
**Effective date:** July 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Internal


## Scope
This policy applies to all business travel and work expenses paid personally and claimed back from Northpeak.

## Meals
| Travel type | Daily meal limit |
|---|---|
| Domestic (within your home country) | **$60 per day** |
| International | **$90 per day** |

Alcohol is not reimbursed, except at customer dinners approved by a director.

## Hotels
- Domestic: up to **$250 per night**.
- Exception: in **New York City, San Francisco and Boston**, up to **$350 per night**.
- International: up to **$300 per night**.

## Flights
Book economy class for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of **6 hours or longer**. Business class requires approval from a vice president before booking.

## Mileage
Using your own car for business is reimbursed at **$0.70 per mile**. Commuting between home and your usual office is not reimbursable.

## Receipts and deadlines
- Itemized receipts are required for every expense over **$25**.
- Submit claims within **30 days** of the expense.
- Claims submitted more than **60 days** after the expense will not be reimbursed.

## Revision history
- 7.0 (July 1, 2026): Meal limits raised from $50 domestic and $75 international to $60 and $90.
- 6.2 (January 1, 2025): Boston added to the high-cost city hotel exception.

## Questions

Contact Finance at expenses@northpeak.example. This policy is reviewed once a year.
