# Corporate Card Policy

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** Finance  
**Version:** 2.0  
**Effective date:** March 1, 2025  
**Classification:** Internal


## Eligibility
Corporate cards are issued to employees who travel for business at least **4 times a year**, and to employees whose role requires regular purchasing, such as office managers.

## Rules
- The card is for business expenses only. Personal use is prohibited, even if you plan to repay it.
- Reconcile card transactions in the expense system within 30 days.
- The same receipt and spending limits as the Travel and Expense Policy apply.

## Lost or stolen cards
Report a lost card to the card provider immediately, then notify expenses@northpeak.example.

## Questions

Contact Finance at expenses@northpeak.example. This policy is reviewed once a year.
