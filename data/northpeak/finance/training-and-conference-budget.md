# Training and Conference Budget

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** People Team and Finance  
**Version:** 2.0  
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Internal


## Annual budget
Each employee has a learning budget of **$2,000 per calendar year** for courses, certifications, books and conference tickets. Unused budget does not roll over.

## Approval
Your manager must approve the spending before you register. Travel to a conference counts against the travel policy, not the learning budget.

## Revision history
- 2.0 (January 1, 2026): Budget increased from $1,500 to $2,000.

## Questions

Contact the People Team at people@northpeak.example. This policy is reviewed once a year.
