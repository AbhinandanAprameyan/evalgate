# Expense and Purchase Approval Limits

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** Finance  
**Version:** 3.3  
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Internal


## Who can approve what
| Amount (single expense or purchase) | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to $2,000 | Your direct manager |
| Over $2,000 up to $10,000 | A director |
| Over $10,000 up to $50,000 | A vice president |
| Over $50,000 | The Chief Financial Officer |

## Rules
- Nobody may approve their own expenses.
- Splitting a purchase into smaller amounts to stay under a limit is a policy violation.
- Approvers are responsible for checking that receipts are attached.

Contact Finance at finance@northpeak.example. This policy is reviewed once a year.
