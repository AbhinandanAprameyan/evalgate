# Procurement and Vendor Policy

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** Finance and Legal  
**Version:** 2.2  
**Effective date:** September 1, 2025  
**Classification:** Internal


## Competitive quotes
Purchases over **$25,000** require at least three written quotes from different vendors, unless only one vendor can supply the item. Record the reason for choosing the vendor.

## New vendors
New vendors must complete a security review if they will handle Northpeak data. Legal must review any contract longer than one year.

## Purchase orders
A purchase order is required before work starts for any vendor engagement over $2,000.

## Questions

Contact Procurement at procurement@northpeak.example. This policy is reviewed once a year.
