# Relocation Assistance

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** People Team and Finance  
**Version:** 1.4  
**Effective date:** January 1, 2025  
**Classification:** Internal


## Who is eligible
Relocation assistance is available to new hires and transferring employees when Northpeak asks them to move to Boston or Austin.

## What is covered
Northpeak reimburses moving costs up to **$10,000**, including movers, one trip to look for housing, and up to 30 days of temporary housing.

## Repayment
If you leave Northpeak voluntarily within 12 months of relocating, you must repay the relocation amount in full.

## Questions

Contact the People Team at people@northpeak.example. This policy is reviewed once a year.
