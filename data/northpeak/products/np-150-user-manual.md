# NP-150 Courier Plus User Manual

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** Product Documentation  
**Version:** 4.2.1  
**Effective date:** July 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Public


## Overview
The NP-150 Courier Plus is an autonomous mobile robot designed for moving totes and small parcels between stations in warehouses, hospitals' supply rooms and factories. It navigates using lidar and cameras, follows a digital map created during installation, and is managed from the Fleet Console.

## Key specifications
| Specification | Value |
|---|---|
| Rated payload | 40 kg |
| Battery runtime (typical) | 12 hours |
| Charging time (empty to full) | 3 hours |
| Maximum speed | 1.8 m/s |
| Robot weight | 45 kg |
| Dust and water protection | IP54 |

## Installation
1. Unpack the robot in a clear area and remove all transport locks (marked in red).
2. Place the charging dock against a wall with at least 1 meter of free space in front of it.
3. Power on the robot and connect it to the site Wi-Fi from the touchscreen.
4. Create the site map by driving the robot manually around the area in Mapping mode.
5. Mark no-go zones and stations in the Fleet Console.

## Daily operation
Check the touchscreen for warnings at the start of each shift. The robot returns to its dock automatically when the battery reaches 15%. Do not exceed the rated payload of 40 kg; overloading can damage the drive system and is not covered by warranty.

## Charging
A full charge takes 3 hours. Opportunity charging (short charges between tasks) is supported and does not harm the battery. The battery should be replaced after 3 years or 1,500 charge cycles, whichever comes first.

## Maintenance
- Clean the lidar window and cameras weekly.
- Required: wheel and caster inspection every 500 operating hours.
- Update firmware when the Fleet Console shows a new release.

## Safety
The robot stops when its emergency stop button is pressed, when a person steps in front of it, or when it detects an unexpected obstacle. Never ride on the robot or place it on slopes steeper than 3 degrees.

## Troubleshooting
See the NP-150 Troubleshooting Guide for error codes. Error codes have different meanings on different Northpeak product families.
