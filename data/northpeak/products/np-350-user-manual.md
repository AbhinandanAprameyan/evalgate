# NP-350 Hauler XL User Manual

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** Product Documentation  
**Version:** 3.9.0  
**Effective date:** July 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Public


## Overview
The NP-350 Hauler XL is an autonomous mobile robot designed for moving pallets and heavy carts across warehouse and factory floors. It navigates using lidar and cameras, follows a digital map created during installation, and is managed from the Fleet Console.

## Key specifications
| Specification | Value |
|---|---|
| Rated payload | 500 kg |
| Battery runtime (typical) | 7 hours |
| Charging time (empty to full) | 4.5 hours |
| Maximum speed | 1.0 m/s |
| Robot weight | 210 kg |
| Dust and water protection | IP44 |

## Installation
1. Unpack the robot in a clear area and remove all transport locks (marked in red).
2. Place the charging dock against a wall with at least 1 meter of free space in front of it.
3. Power on the robot and connect it to the site Wi-Fi from the touchscreen.
4. Create the site map by driving the robot manually around the area in Mapping mode.
5. Mark no-go zones and stations in the Fleet Console.

## Daily operation
Check the touchscreen for warnings at the start of each shift. The robot returns to its dock automatically when the battery reaches 15%. Do not exceed the rated payload of 500 kg; overloading can damage the drive system and is not covered by warranty.

## Charging
A full charge takes 4.5 hours. Opportunity charging (short charges between tasks) is supported and does not harm the battery. The battery should be replaced after 4 years or 2,000 charge cycles, whichever comes first.

## Maintenance
- Clean the lidar window and cameras weekly.
- Required: lift actuator inspection every 250 operating hours.
- Update firmware when the Fleet Console shows a new release.

## Safety
The robot stops when its emergency stop button is pressed, when a person steps in front of it, or when it detects an unexpected obstacle. Never ride on the robot or place it on slopes steeper than 3 degrees.

## Troubleshooting
See the NP-350 Troubleshooting Guide for error codes. Error codes have different meanings on different Northpeak product families.
