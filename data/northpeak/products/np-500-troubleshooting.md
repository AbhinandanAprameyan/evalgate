# NP-500 Picker Troubleshooting Guide

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** Customer Support  
**Version:** 3.0  
**Effective date:** July 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Public


These error codes apply to **Picker family robots** (NP-500, NP-550). Other Northpeak product families use the same codes with different meanings.

| Code | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| E101 | Gripper vacuum pressure low | Check the suction cups for tears and the vacuum hose for disconnections; replace worn gripper pads. |
| E102 | Camera calibration expired | Run Camera Calibration from the Fleet Console using the supplied calibration board. |
| E103 | Item not detected in pick zone | Check that the item is inside the marked pick zone and that the lighting is not too dim. |
| E104 | Arm collision detected | Clear the obstruction, check the arm for damage, and press Resume. Report repeated collisions to support. |
| E105 | Joint temperature too high | Pause picking for 20 minutes and check that the arm cooling fans are running. |

If a code appears three times in one shift, stop using the robot and contact Northpeak support at 1-800-555-0199.
