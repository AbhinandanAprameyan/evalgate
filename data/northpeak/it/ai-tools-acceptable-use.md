# Acceptable Use of AI Tools

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** IT Security and Legal  
**Version:** 1.1  
**Effective date:** March 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Internal


## Approved tools
Northpeak Assist, the internal assistant, is approved for questions about company policies and products. It answers from company documents and does not send data outside Northpeak.

## Public AI tools
You may use public AI chat tools for general questions, but you must never paste Confidential or Restricted information into them, including customer data, source code from private repositories, or unreleased product plans.

## Checking AI answers
AI assistants can make mistakes. For decisions involving money, safety, legal obligations or customers, confirm the answer in the source document or with the policy owner.

## Questions

Contact IT Security at security@northpeak.example. This policy is reviewed once a year.
