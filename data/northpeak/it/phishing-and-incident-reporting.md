# Phishing and Security Incident Reporting

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** IT Security  
**Version:** 2.0  
**Effective date:** February 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Internal


## Suspicious emails
Do not click links or open attachments in suspicious emails. Forward the email as an attachment to **phish@northpeak.example**, then delete it.

## If you clicked something
Report it immediately to IT Security at extension **4357** (option 1). You will not be punished for reporting a mistake quickly.

## Gift card scams
Northpeak executives will never ask you to buy gift cards or send money urgently by email or text. Treat any such request as a scam.

## Questions

Contact IT Security at security@northpeak.example. This policy is reviewed once a year.
