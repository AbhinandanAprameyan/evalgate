# Password and Multi-Factor Authentication Policy

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** IT Security  
**Version:** 3.0  
**Effective date:** April 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Internal


## Password requirements
- Passwords must be at least **14 characters** long.
- Use a passphrase of several unrelated words rather than a short complex password.
- Never reuse a Northpeak password on another site.
- Store passwords in VaultKey, the company password manager.

Passwords no longer need to be changed on a schedule. Change your password only if you suspect it has been exposed, or if IT Security asks you to.

## Multi-factor authentication (MFA)
MFA is required for every company account. Use the authenticator app; text message codes are not allowed for administrator accounts.

## Revision history
- 3.0 (April 1, 2026): Minimum length raised from 12 characters to 14 characters; mandatory 90-day password changes removed.

## Questions

Contact IT Security at security@northpeak.example. This policy is reviewed once a year.
