# Device and Laptop Policy

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** IT  
**Version:** 4.0  
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Internal


## Company laptops
Every employee receives a company laptop (a MacBook Pro or a ThinkPad, depending on role). Laptops are replaced every **3 years**, or sooner if they fail.

## Personal devices
Personal phones may be used for email and chat only after installing the company device-management profile. Personal computers may not be used to access internal systems.

## Software
Only approved software may be installed. Request new software through an IT ticket; requests are answered within **2 business days**.

## Revision history
- 4.0 (January 1, 2026): Laptop replacement cycle shortened from 4 years to 3 years.

## Questions

Contact IT at it@northpeak.example. This policy is reviewed once a year.
