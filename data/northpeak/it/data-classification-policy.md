# Data Classification Policy

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** IT Security and Legal  
**Version:** 2.0  
**Effective date:** January 1, 2025  
**Classification:** Internal


## Four levels of data
| Level | Examples | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| Public | Website content, published product specs | May be shared freely |
| Internal | Most policies, meeting notes, org charts | Employees only |
| Confidential | Customer contracts, unreleased product plans, reorganization plans | Only people who need it for their work |
| Restricted | Salary data, personal employee records, merger and acquisition plans | Named individuals only; never shared through chat tools or assistants |

## Labels
Every document must show its classification near the top. If a document has no label, treat it as Confidential.

## Sharing outside Northpeak
Confidential and Restricted data may only be shared outside Northpeak under a signed non-disclosure agreement and with approval from the data owner.

Contact IT Security at security@northpeak.example. This policy is reviewed once a year.
