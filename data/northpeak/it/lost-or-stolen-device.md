# Lost or Stolen Devices

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** IT Security  
**Version:** 2.1  
**Effective date:** May 1, 2025  
**Classification:** Internal


## What to do
If a company laptop, phone or security key is lost or stolen:
1. Report it to IT Security **within 1 hour** of noticing, by calling extension **4357** (option 1) or emailing security@northpeak.example.
2. If it was stolen, file a police report and send the report number to IT Security.
3. Do not try to recover the device yourself.

IT Security will lock and remotely erase the device. Reporting quickly matters more than knowing exactly what happened.

## Questions

Contact IT Security at security@northpeak.example. This policy is reviewed once a year.
