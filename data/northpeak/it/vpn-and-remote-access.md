# VPN and Remote Access

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** IT  
**Version:** 2.3  
**Effective date:** October 1, 2025  
**Classification:** Internal


## When the VPN is required
Connect to the Northpeak VPN whenever you access internal systems (the Fleet Console, HR portal, source code or file shares) from outside a Northpeak office. Email and chat work without the VPN.

## Public Wi-Fi
On public Wi-Fi, connect to the VPN before doing any work.

## Problems connecting
Restart the VPN client first. If it still fails, open an IT ticket or call the help desk.

## Questions

Contact IT at it@northpeak.example. This policy is reviewed once a year.
