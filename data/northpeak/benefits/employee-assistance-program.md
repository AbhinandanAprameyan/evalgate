# Employee Assistance Program

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** People Team  
**Version:** 1.2  
**Effective date:** January 1, 2025  
**Classification:** Internal


## What it offers
The Employee Assistance Program offers free, confidential counseling sessions (up to six per issue per year), plus legal and financial consultations, for employees and their household members.

## How to access it
Call the program's 24-hour line listed in the HR portal. Northpeak never receives information about who uses the service.

## Questions

Contact Benefits at benefits@northpeak.example. This policy is reviewed once a year.
