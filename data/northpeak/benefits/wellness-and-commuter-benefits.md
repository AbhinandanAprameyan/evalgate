# Wellness and Commuter Benefits

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** People Team  
**Version:** 2026  
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Internal


## Wellness stipend
Every employee receives a wellness stipend of **$600 per year** for gym memberships, fitness classes, sports equipment or mental-health apps.

## Commuter benefits
- **Boston:** Northpeak pays **50% of a monthly MBTA transit pass, up to $100 per month**. There is no company parking at the Boston office.
- **Austin:** parking in the office garage is free for all employees.
- **Remote employees:** commuter benefits do not apply.

## Revision history
- 2026: Wellness stipend increased from $400 to $600; Boston transit subsidy increased from 30% up to $60 to 50% up to $100.

## Questions

Contact Benefits at benefits@northpeak.example. This policy is reviewed once a year.
