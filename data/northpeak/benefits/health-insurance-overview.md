# Health Insurance Overview

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** People Team  
**Version:** 2026  
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Internal


## Plans
Northpeak offers three medical plans. The company pays part of the monthly premium:

| Plan | Company share of employee-only premium | Company share of family premium |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | 100% | 80% |
| Plus | 90% | 75% |
| Premier | 80% | 70% |

Dental and vision coverage are included with every plan.

## Enrollment
New employees must enroll within **30 days** of their start date. Otherwise, you can only change plans during open enrollment in November, or after a qualifying life event such as marriage or the birth of a child.
