# 401(k) Retirement Plan

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** People Team  
**Version:** 2026  
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Internal


## Company match
Northpeak matches **100% of your contributions up to 4% of your salary**. Matching contributions vest immediately, so they are yours from day one.

## Enrolling
New employees are automatically enrolled at 3% after 30 days unless they choose a different rate or opt out. You can change your contribution rate at any time.

## Upcoming change
An increase to the company match has been announced for future years. Until it takes effect, the current match described above applies.

## Questions

Contact Benefits at benefits@northpeak.example. This policy is reviewed once a year.
