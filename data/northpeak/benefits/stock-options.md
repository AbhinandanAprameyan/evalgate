# Employee Stock Options

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** People Team and Finance  
**Version:** 2.0  
**Effective date:** January 1, 2025  
**Classification:** Internal


## Vesting
Stock options granted to employees vest over **4 years** with a **1 year cliff**: no options vest during the first year, then 25% vest at the one-year anniversary and the rest vest monthly over the following three years.

## Leaving Northpeak
You have 90 days after leaving to exercise vested options. Unvested options are returned to the company.

## Questions about your grant
Grant details are available in the equity portal. Individual grant amounts are Restricted information.

## Questions

Contact the Equity team at equity@northpeak.example. This policy is reviewed once a year.
