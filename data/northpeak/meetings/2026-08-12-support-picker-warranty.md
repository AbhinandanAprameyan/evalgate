# Customer Support Update: Picker Warranty

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Date:** August 12, 2026  
**Classification:** Internal

Picker family robots (NP-500, NP-550) **delivered on or after July 1, 2026** now come with a **3-year warranty**. Picker robots delivered before July 1, 2026 keep their original 2-year warranty.
