# NP-350 Hauler XL Frequently Asked Questions

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** Customer Support  
**Version:** 2026  
**Effective date:** July 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Public


**How much can the Hauler XL carry?** Up to 500 kg.

**How long does the battery last?** About 7 hours per charge under typical conditions, less with heavy loads or in the cold.

**Can it work outdoors?** No. The NP-350 is rated IP44 and is designed for indoor use only.

**How long is the warranty?** See the Warranty Policy. Batteries are covered for 1 year.

**Can I mix Hauler XL robots with other Northpeak models?** Yes, all models run in the same Fleet Console.

**Where do I find the serial number?** On the label under the charging port.
