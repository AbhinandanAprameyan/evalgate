# Refund and Return Policy

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** Customer Support  
**Version:** 3.0  
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Public


## Standard returns
Customers may return a robot within **30 days of delivery**.
- **Unopened** robots receive a full refund.
- **Opened or deployed** robots receive a refund minus a **15% restocking fee**.
- **Custom-configured** robots (custom paint, custom attachments or customer-specific firmware) cannot be returned.

## Enterprise contracts
Orders of **10 or more robots** are governed by the customer's enterprise contract, which replaces this policy.

## Return shipping by region
| Region | Who pays return shipping |
|---|---|
| United States | Northpeak pays for defective units; customer pays otherwise |
| Canada | Same as the United States; import duties are not refunded |
| European Union | Customer pays return shipping; VAT is refunded |

## Revision history
- 3.0 (January 1, 2026): 15% restocking fee introduced for opened robots.

## Questions
