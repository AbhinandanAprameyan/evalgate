# Warranty Policy

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** Customer Support  
**Version:** 4.0  
**Effective date:** July 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Public


## Standard warranty
| Product | Warranty |
|---|---|
| Courier family (NP-100, NP-150) | 2 years from delivery |
| Hauler family (NP-300, NP-350) | 2 years from delivery |
| Picker family (NP-500, NP-550), delivered on or after July 1, 2026 | 3 years from delivery |
| Picker family, delivered before July 1, 2026 | 2 years from delivery |
| Batteries (all models) | 1 year |

## Extended warranty
Customers can add 2 extra years of coverage if they buy the extended warranty within **90 days** of delivery.

## Not covered
Damage from overloading beyond rated payload, water exposure beyond the robot's protection rating, unauthorized repairs, and normal wear parts such as wheels and gripper pads.

## Revision history
- 4.0 (July 1, 2026): Picker warranty extended to 3 years for units delivered on or after July 1, 2026.
- 3.0 (January 1, 2025): Courier and Hauler warranty extended from 1 year to 2 years.

## Questions

Contact Customer Support at support@northpeak.example. This policy is reviewed once a year.
