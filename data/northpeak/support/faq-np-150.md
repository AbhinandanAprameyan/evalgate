# NP-150 Courier Plus Frequently Asked Questions

**Company:** Northpeak Robotics  
**Owner:** Customer Support  
**Version:** 2026  
**Effective date:** July 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Public


**How much can the Courier Plus carry?** Up to 40 kg.

**How long does the battery last?** About 12 hours per charge under typical conditions, less with heavy loads or in the cold.

**Can it work outdoors?** No. The NP-150 is rated IP54 and is designed for indoor use only.

**How long is the warranty?** See the Warranty Policy. Batteries are covered for 1 year.

**Can I mix Courier Plus robots with other Northpeak models?** Yes, all models run in the same Fleet Console.

**Where do I find the serial number?** On the label under the charging port.
